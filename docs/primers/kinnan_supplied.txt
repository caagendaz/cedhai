Overview
Kinnan NBC is an alternative approach to Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy. This list runs much fewer disruption creatures / “big flips“ and instead focuses on drawing cards more consistently. Copy Enchantment, Mirrormade and Clever Impersonator are considered additional copies of Rhystic Study / Mystic Remora, increasing our access to the most powerful draw engines in the game. If you like layered combos, big mana, and drawing cards, this list might be right for you.

Strategy
This list is built with an larger emphasis on card advantage engines through enchantment clones like Mirrormade, Copy Enchantment, Clever Impersonator, and Astral Dragon. These cards allow us to get Mystic Remora and Rhystic Study into play more consistently even without unconditional tutors. Our other primary focus is to leverage Kinnan's mana-doubling ability to establish early big mana sources to provide early explosive turns.

While the deck is capable of executing fast combos, it thrives on patience and timing. We want to draw cards until the ideal window presents itself. Spinning Kinnan’s ability to cheat high-value permanents into play serves as a backup plan (unless we have Seedborn Muse, which allows us to repeatedly activate Kinnan on every opponent’s turn for overwhelming advantage). By focusing on resource accumulation and carefully picking our window, the deck balances resilience and explosiveness, ensuring we can pivot smoothly from card advantage to a decisive win when the opportunity arises.

Faerie Mastermind +Smothering Tithe
Faerie Mastermind Smothering Tithe
Requirements: Faerie Mastermind, Smothering Tithe, and Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy in play + .
You can copy an opponent's Smothering Tithe with Clever Impersonator, Copy Enchantment, or Mirrormade.

Use 2 treasure tokens to activate Faerie Mastermind (Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy makes them each produce an additional ).
Resolve your Smothering Tithe triggers, creating 3 treasure tokens.
Repeat steps 1 and 2 (netting an additional treasure token each time) to draw cards until each player has drawn most of their deck.
Resolve Endurance or continuously cast Green Sun's Zenith to refill your library while decking your opponents.
Timing:

This line is best used at instant speed when your Smothering Tithe triggers will resolve before your opponents' Smothering Tithe triggers. You can guarantee this by starting the combo on the turn of the player to your left.
You should do this went your opponents do not have a large amount of mana available. This is so their interaction will be limited by mana and you will be able to interact with every spell in your deck.
Execution:

This line is very hard to interrupt once you have the mana available to activate Faerie Mastermind multiple times. This is because you can respond to removal spells with more activations, never letting the removal spells resolve.
Once you've created a large amount of mana, you can begin spinning Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy to put some important creatures into play. These include Valley Floodcaller, High Fae Trickster, Hullbreaker Horror, Endurance. Make sure to check which creatures are still in your deck before activating.
Before trying to resolve Endurance or Green Sun's Zenith, it is best to make your opponents fight over other important cards like Hullbreaker Horror, High Fae Trickster, Valley Floodcaller, Crop Rotation, Chord of Calling, etc. This will help you fight through all their interaction by trading payoff spells for their counterspells. It is easy to win this battle if you have the mana advantage, and even easier if you have an instant speed enabler in play.

Note: We will assume we have access to all the cards in our deck with another infinite combo. Ensure you have access to the majority of your counterspells before starting this loop.

You can always bounce and recast Endurance as many times as necessary to refill your library and reuse counterspells.

Mulligans
Mulligan Guide
We are looking for 3 primary things in our mulligans:

Draw Engines
Early Win Attempt
Mana
The strength of Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy is it's ability to pivot gameplans easily. If a mulligan has tons of mana, we can activate Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy to find a gameplan. If we have a draw engine, we'll likely draw into mana development. We can also go for early wins with Basalt Monolith that our opponents might not be ready for.

In these mulligan examples we will not choose turn order or pod composition - they will discussed when they are relevant to the mulligan decision.

UPDATE IN PROGRESS
Hand 1
Chord of CallingMystic RemoraVeil of SummerSol RingOtawara Talisman of Curiosity Tropical Island

Keep. This hand is an amazing hand in any seat of just about any pod. Turn 1 Sol Ring Talisman of Curiosity into Mystic Remora means you'll not only have card draw on turn 1 but also mana to pay for Mystic Remora while developing your board. This hand is what dreams are made of.

Hand 2
Mulligan 2
Turn Order: Rograkh Silas, Tymna Kraum, Tymna Thrasios, Kinnan
Cephalid Coliseum, Mystic Remora, Valley Floodcaller, Commandeer, Gemstone Caverns, Boseiju, Who Endures, The One Ring

Keep. Mystic Remora is exactly what we want to see, especially with any mana acceleration like Gemstone Caverns. Pitch The One Ring to Gemstone Caverns so you can guarantee land drops and hold up Commandeer after drawing more blue cards from Mystic Remora. We'll aim to take a Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, or payoff from Rograkh Silas with Commandeer.

Hand 3
Mulligan 3
Turn Order: Tymna Kraum, Kinnan, Rograkh Silas, Tymna Thrasios
Worldly Tutor, Mox Diamond, Commandeer, Ancient Tomb, Rhystic Study, Delighted Halfling, Swan Song

Mulligan. While drawing a land gives us the huge upside of turn 1 Rhystic Study, the downside of missing our 1st land drop is huge. This hand is more keepable on a mull to 4 (keeping Mox Diamond, Ancient Tomb, Rhystic Study, and Delighted Halfling) because a 3 is not likely to be better than the potential this hand has.

Hand 4
Mulligan 4
Turn Order: Tymna Thrasios, Kinnan, Tymna Kraum, Rograkh Silas
Forbidden Orchard, Lotus Petal, Mystic Remora, Mindbreak Trap, Tezzeret the Seeker, Arcane Signet, Astral Dragon

Keep. Turn 1 Arcane Signet into Mystic Remora is always a strong opening, especially when going 2nd. We're also happy to see Mindbreak Trap in our opening hand to stop an early win attempt.

Hand 5
Mulligan 5
Turn Order: Tymna Kraum, Tymna Thrasios, Rograkh Silas, Kinnan
Spellskite, Exotic Orchard, Command Tower, The One Ring, Fyndhorn Elves, Delighted Halfling, Valley Floodcaller

Mulligan. This hand is keepable, but going last with the plan of turn 3 The One Ring isn't idea. We'll go turn 1 Fyndhorn Elves, turn 2 Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy and Delighted Halfling, and turn 3 The One Ring drawing 1 card. The game might be over by this point, so we can likely do better. This hand is probably a keep on a mull to 4 or 5.

Hand 6
Mulligan 6
Turn Order: Tymna Kraum, Kinnan, Tymna Thrasios, Rograkh Silas
Simic Signet, Breeding Pool, The One Ring, Nyxbloom Ancient, Dramatic Reversal, Cephalid Coliseum, Lotus Petal

Mulligan. While it looks like this hand has a lot of mana, it doesn't play out as well as we'd like. We can go turn 1 Simic Signet using our Lotus Petal into turn 2 Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy, but this just leaves use with 4 mana on turn 3 to tap out for The One Ring. That can get us there, but we can do better on a 7.

Hand 7
Mulligan 7
Turn Order: Kinnan, Tymna Thrasios, Tymna Kraum, Rograkh Silas
Bloom Tender, Seedborn Muse, Sink into Stupor, Clever Impersonator, Flooded Strand, Elvish Spirit Guide, Springleaf Drum

Keep. We can cast Bloom Tender turn 1 using Elvish Spirit Guide. Turn 2 we'll cast Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy into Springleaf Drum, giving us 4 mana for Clever Impersonator to hopefully copy a Rhystic Study or Mystic Remora. If those aren't on the field, we can use it to make more mana by copying a mana rock or Bloom Tender to setup for a turn 3 Seedborn Muse.
